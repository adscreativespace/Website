3. Enquiries and consultations

6. Scope, revisions and changes

  • Every package includes two rounds of revisions at each design stage. Further revisions are charged extra.
  • Changes to an approved design (for example a new layout after the plan is approved, an added floor, or a change of use) are a change in scope and are quoted separately.
  • Unless listed in your quotation, the following are not included: structural design and drawings, MEP (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) design, soil tests and surveys, government and municipal approvals, statutory fees, and landscape design.
  • Working drawings are architectural drawings. Structural drawings must be prepared and certified by a qualified structural engineer.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information: site dimensions, land documents, setbacks, local rules you know of, budget and requirements.
  • Give approvals and feedback in good time. Delays in approvals, payments or information extend the timeline.
  • Obtain all permissions needed for construction (building plan sanction, NOCs and similar) unless we have agreed in writing to assist with them.
  • Check measurements on site before construction or fabrication. Figured dimensions take precedence over scaled ones.

  • Any execution or coordination work we take on is covered by its own written agreement and quotation.
