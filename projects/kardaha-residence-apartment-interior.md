This 1,500 sq.ft. apartment in Kardaha was planned as one continuous space, from the foyer through the living room to the dining area and kitchen, with each zone defined by finishes and lighting rather than walls.

A single material language ties the home together: chevron oak with fine brass inlays on every door, wall panel and the TV wall, balanced by white wainscot mouldings and a marble-look floor. In the living room, a large backlit onyx-look panel framed by chevron oak and brass ring lights becomes the focal point.

The dining area is the heart of the plan. A teal glass chandelier hangs over a glass-top table, in front of a lit crockery unit that wraps around a wash basin and mirror. A slatted oak partition screens the kitchen while keeping the space open and connected.
