Riyas Makeover brings three services under one roof in Kaliaganj: a ladies parlour, a bridal makeup studio and a private spa room. Within 450 sq.ft., each zone was given its own character, while a shared material palette of terrazzo, light wood and soft white keeps the brand experience consistent from reception to treatment.

The parlour is bright and practical. Arched styling mirrors with backlit product niches line one wall, a teal slatted ceiling marks the shampoo station, and a sculpted partition with pill-shaped cut-outs separates the waiting lounge from the styling floor. The reception counter and recessed shelving double as a retail display for hair and skin care products.

The makeup studio takes a more theatrical turn for bridal and party clients, with a hand-painted Indian heritage mural, Hollywood bulb mirrors, velvet seating, red drapes and a brass chandelier. The spa room, by contrast, is calm and enclosed, with textured walls, warm wood joinery and soft lighting for facials and treatments.
