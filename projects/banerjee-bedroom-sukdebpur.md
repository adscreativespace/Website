Designed for Prasun Banerjee in Sukdebpur, this 150 sq.ft. bedroom shows how a compact room can hold sleeping, storage and study without feeling crowded.

A wall-to-wall teal upholstered headboard anchors the bed wall, framed by white panels with concealed LED strips. Opposite, a single built-in unit combines a full-height wardrobe, a study niche with a floating desk and lit shelving, and a matching door, so storage and work space take up one wall instead of three.

A soft palette of teal, white and light oak, together with a cove-lit ceiling and marble-look flooring, keeps the room bright, calm and visually larger than its footprint.
