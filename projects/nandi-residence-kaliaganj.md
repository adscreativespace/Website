Designed for Pratik Nandi in Kaliaganj, this G+2 residence was taken from floor planning through to construction drawings. Each floor is planned as a compact three-bedroom home of roughly 1,200 sq.ft., with bedrooms at the front and rear, an open living and dining area at the centre, and the staircase set to one side.

Alongside the architectural plans, ADS Creative Space prepared the foundation, column and tie beam layouts on a coordinated 12-column grid that follows the plot. The building is currently under construction.
