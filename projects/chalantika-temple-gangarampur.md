Designed for the Chalantika Mandir Committee on High Road, Gangarampur, this community temple brings two sanctums, a congregation space and supporting facilities together on a compact 50' × 50' site. ADS Creative Space prepared the architecture, planning and complete structural design.

The temple is raised on a podium about 2.5 m above the road and reached by twin staircases, so the sanctum sits above street level and festival crowds can move freely. Above, a cluster of five ribbed shikharas with gilded kalash finials gives the temple a strong presence on the street, while white marble-finish surfaces with pietra dura floral inlay bring fine detail to the pillars and walls. A three-storey side block houses ancillary rooms, toilets and a wash area.
