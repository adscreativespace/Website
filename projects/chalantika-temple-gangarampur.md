Designed for the Chalantika Mandir Committee on High Road, Gangarampur, this community temple brings two sanctums, a congregation space and guest accommodation together on a compact 50' × 50' site. ADS Creative Space prepared the architecture, planning and complete structural design, and the temple is now under construction.

During the puja, devotees, sadhus and gurus travel here from across West Bengal and other parts of India, so the side block was planned as guest rooms with toilets and a wash area, letting visitors stay on site. The temple itself is raised on a podium about 2.5 m above the road and reached by twin staircases, and a cluster of five ribbed shikharas with gilded kalash finials and pietra dura floral inlay gives it a strong presence on High Road.
