Designed for the Chalantika Mandir Committee on High Road, Gangarampur, Chalantika Matri Mandir brings two sanctums, a congregation space and guest accommodation together on a compact 50' × 50' site. ADS Creative Space prepared the architecture, planning and complete structural design, and the temple is now under construction.

The building is planned in two parts. The main temple is G+1, raised on a podium about 2.5 m above the road, reached by twin staircases and crowned by a cluster of five ribbed shikharas with gilded kalash finials and pietra dura floral inlay. Alongside it, a G+3 guest block hosts the devotees, sadhus and gurus who travel here for the puja from across West Bengal and other parts of India.
