Designed for Ghosh Healthcare in Gangarampur, this 600 sq.ft. CT scan suite brings together a scan room and its control room in a layout that works as well for patients as it does for clinical staff.

The scan room is planned around the gantry, with clear circulation for staff and stretcher access, a modular ceiling carrying lighting and air diffusers, and smooth, easy-to-clean finishes suited to a diagnostic environment. To ease patient anxiety, a floor-to-ceiling tropical beach mural wraps the walls in the patient's view, softening what is usually a stark clinical space.

The adjoining control room looks directly onto the scanner through a timber-framed viewing window, giving technicians constant visual contact with the patient. A two-person workstation with ergonomic seating, overhead storage and task lighting completes a practical, comfortable workspace.
