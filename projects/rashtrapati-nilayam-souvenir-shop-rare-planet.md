ADS Creative Space has designed 34 retail stores for RARE PLANET and its convenience brand, 2M Minimart, at heritage sites and airports across India. The flagship store at Rashtrapati Nilayam, Secunderabad, was inaugurated by the Hon'ble President of India in December 2024.

Each store works within a common brand system of backlit timber fascias, lit modular shelving and flexible display fixtures, while taking on a local identity through materials and motifs, from carved timber in Mumbai and copper jaali in Ranchi to Kashmiri patterns in Srinagar.
