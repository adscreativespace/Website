ADS Creative Space designed the interior of the RARE PLANET souvenir shop at Rashtrapati Nilayam, Secunderabad, the President of India's southern retreat. The store was inaugurated by the Hon'ble President of India, Smt. Droupadi Murmu, in December 2024.

The brief was a compact retail space that could present a large and varied range of souvenirs clearly, within a heritage setting that receives a steady flow of visitors. The design uses a full-height glass shopfront and a backlit fascia carrying a line drawing of Rashtrapati Nilayam, so the store is visible and recognisable from the corridor.

Inside, light timber shelving within slim white frames and warm concealed lighting create a calm, open backdrop that lets the products lead. The modular display system can be reconfigured as the collection changes.

At Srinagar Airport, the same brand is adapted for travel retail: a deep blue portal with white jaali pillars inspired by Kashmiri patterns, a backlit timber fascia, and black metal racks with lit timber shelves against a patterned back panel. Pegboard walls, a gondola tower and grid-mesh units give the compact store flexible ways to display small souvenirs.

At Ayodhya Airport, the brand becomes a compact, themed kiosk: a backlit timber fascia with the "Jai Shree Ram" greeting, large-format artwork of Lord Rama on the side elevation and lit diya motifs on the pillars, reflecting the city's identity for arriving visitors.

For 2M Minimart, RARE PLANET's convenience brand, the Nagpur Airport store takes a brighter, faster approach: teal and white shelving around the perimeter, a mustard brand wall with travel icons, a central counter between two gondola towers and a display chiller for grab-and-go purchases.

At Trivandrum Airport, a second 2M Minimart pairs a yellow icon-printed portal with light oak and bright green shelving, a double-sided lightbox sign and a drinks chiller, giving the brand a cheerful, high-visibility presence in the terminal.

At Ranchi Airport, the RARE PLANET kiosk is framed by two copper-finish jaali pillars, each a stack of different laser-cut patterns that glow as light passes through them, turning a compact kiosk into a striking landmark in the terminal.

At Vizag Airport, copper jaali panels and a softly lit paisley back wall frame black-framed glass display tables and a central gondola, giving the store a warm, decorative character.

At Vijayawada Airport, a deep, narrow unit is organised around one central aisle, with brass jaali pillars at the entrance, lit shelving along both walls and glass display units down the middle.

At Bhopal Airport, a third 2M Minimart pairs a backlit yellow icon fascia with warm timber shelving, black uprights with LED strips, a slatted gondola and a drinks chiller.

At Mangalore Airport, copper jaali pillars frame a lighter store with grey metal racks, pale timber shelves and storage, a patterned back wall, a grid display wall and an integrated cash desk.

At Pune Airport, a wide open-front store pairs a deep backlit timber fascia and timber jaali pillars with black racks and lit shelving on three sides, a grid display wall and a central counter flanked by glass display units.

At Mumbai Airport, the brand takes its most expressive form: an open island kiosk entered through a carved timber gateway with lattice borders and bell motifs, surrounded by a curved arc of carved timber-and-glass display cabinets.

At Varanasi Airport, copper jaali pillars frame a compact store of light oak shelving with soft backlit arches, a cluster of pendant lights and a perforated metal cash counter. Finish options in light oak and dark walnut were developed for the client.

At Bangalore Airport Terminal 1, a wide open-front store under a patterned fascia organises a full product range into clearly signed categories, from yoga and stationery to toys, snacks and chocolates, with vertical timber fins and a digital display panel at the entrance.

These are fourteen of the 34 RARE PLANET stores designed by ADS Creative Space.
