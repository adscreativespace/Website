Designed for Asim Chatterjee in Indranarayanpur Colony, Gangarampur, this project covers the complete first floor of an independent house: around 2,500 sq.ft. of entrance, living, kitchen, dining and four bedrooms.

The home opens with a timber-wrapped entrance and a carved wood-finish main door, with a Panchmukhi Hanuman relief in a lit niche above. Inside, the living room is calm and light, organised around a Buddha console, while the kitchen and dining area pair glossy black units with a green marble table and a lit fluted wall.

Each of the four bedrooms has its own identity: copper leaf, green wave, walnut jaali and stone geometric. All four share floating beds with LED bases, backlit feature walls behind the bed and full-height built-in storage, so the home feels varied but consistent.
