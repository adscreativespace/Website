This bedroom was planned as a self-contained suite for living, working and getting ready, not just for sleeping. Instead of building solid walls, we used a rose-gold framed fluted-glass partition to divide the room. It screens the dressing zone and still lets light and views pass through.

The curved bed is set against a concrete-textured feature wall framed by a soft-cornered, cove-lit recess. A watercolour botanical wall faces the bed and holds the TV. Across the room, a bouclé loveseat and a marble-and-brass table sit under a backlit sculptural relief, creating a quiet reading and conversation corner.

The study and wardrobe use a shared design language. Powder-blue fluted panelling, arched motifs and LED-lit open niches tie the joinery together. The dressing zone ends in an organic-shaped mirror over a curved floating vanity. Fluted glass shutters keep storage light and easy to see into.

Curves repeat across the ceiling, bed, mirror, desk and wardrobe arches. This gives the room a calm, continuous feel while each zone keeps its own clear purpose.
