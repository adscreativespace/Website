Designed for the Niresh Paul residence in Gangarampur, this 230 sq.ft. living room balances everyday comfort with a strong design identity. It brings together seating, entertainment, storage and a pooja niche in a welcoming space for family and guests, without making the compact room feel crowded.

A flowing wave ceiling with concealed lighting sets the tone, while marble-finish feature walls behind the sofa and TV unit are framed by chevron wood panelling with brass inlays. All wood surfaces are finished in ICA polyester coating for a rich, durable finish that holds up to daily use.

A sage-grey palette ties the sofa, TV unit, storage and fluted panelling together. An arched, backlit pooja niche is built into the sofa wall, and black steel-framed glass doors connect the living room to the rest of the home while keeping it a defined, intimate space.
