Designed for Bishu Dey Sarkar in Balurghat, this 3,000 sq.ft. residence is a complete home interior, covering the foyer, a ground-floor formal living room, an upstairs family living room, a prayer niche, dining, kitchen and three bedrooms.

The home opens with a statement foyer: a carved entrance door, a cast-stone water wall, a vertical garden and a timber arched portal that frames the view inside. From there, each space takes on its own character. The formal living room is soft and curved with a statement chandelier fan, while the family living room upstairs is warmer, with an upholstered panel wall, brass accents and a corridor leading to a calm, arched prayer niche.

Dining and kitchen are planned as a connected unit, with a slatted timber ceiling and mirror wall over the dining table, a fully equipped U-shaped kitchen and a lit crockery unit between them. The three bedrooms each have a distinct mood, from the master bedroom's sculptural relief wall and walnut ceiling, to a botanical mural with a built-in study, to a calm wave-line feature wall.

Recurring arches, warm timber, marble and layered lighting tie the whole home together into one cohesive design.
