Designed for Dr. Sukanta Manna's residence in Balurghat, this project covers two bedrooms, each with its own palette and personality, unified by clean contemporary detailing, premium finishes and well-planned built-in storage.

The master bedroom embraces a soft sage green palette with peacock chinoiserie wallpaper, an upholstered headboard, custom wardrobes and a comfortable window seat. Layered lighting, clean architectural lines and warm metallic accents create a peaceful retreat with understated luxury.

The second bedroom shifts to a fresh dusty-blue and white scheme. A channel-tufted headboard sits beneath sculpted gold leaf wall art, botanical Roman blinds soften the windows, and a floating study desk with lit shelving is built in alongside full-height wardrobes, making the room as practical for work and study as it is restful.
