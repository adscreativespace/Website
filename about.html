Our Philosophy

We believe good design starts with the people who will use a space, and only works if it can be built well, on budget and on time.

Our work covers homes, hotels, healthcare facilities, temples and airport retail stores. Each project is planned from concept to execution-ready drawings, with architecture, structure and interiors coordinated in one place.

Rooted in North Bengal, we understand the local climate, materials and craftsmanship, and bring the same rigour to projects across India.

FOUNDER

Atish Dipankar Basak

Founder & Principal Consultant

With a multidisciplinary foundation in Architecture, Civil Engineering, Industrial Engineering & Management, and Interior Design, Atish Dipankar Basak founded ADS Creative Space with a vision to create thoughtful, innovative, and timeless spaces that balance aesthetics, functionality, and buildability.

Beyond professional practice, Atish is an avid traveller with a deep appreciation for mountains, cultural heritage, and historic architecture. His journeys across India and destinations such as Malaysia and Vietnam are opportunities to explore the relationship between architecture, culture, materials, and craftsmanship. From the intricate details of ancient and religious structures to the distinctive character of regional architecture, he finds inspiration in observing how places reflect the people, traditions, and environments that shape them.

This spirit of exploration informs his design perspective, encouraging a deeper understanding of architectural details, material expression, and context-sensitive design. At ADS Creative Space, he brings together professional expertise and a continuing curiosity about the built environment to create spaces that are meaningful, functional, and enduring.

Notable work includes the Rashtrapati Nilayam Souvenir Shop in Secunderabad and 34 airport stores across India for RARE PLANET and 2M Minimart. Every project is personally overseen, with a planning assistant supporting drawings and documentation.
